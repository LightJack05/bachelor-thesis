The following chapter provides an introduction to the proposed solution.
The first section will give an overview of the scope, in order to ensure that given the limited time available for implementation, the resulting product is able to be finished on time.
Following sections will then detail the concept for how management of the Ansible inventory, \texttt{Playbooks}, \texttt{Roles}, and other entities will work, what parameters these objects may use and what their relations are.
Following that there will be detail on how the \texttt{Playbooks} will be executed and how credentials and other requirements are provided to Ansible.

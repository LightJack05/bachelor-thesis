In order to provide an overview of what the goals and the underlying problem of this thesis are, the following chapter will introduce readers to the respective concepts.
The first section will introduce the definition of the problem with managing diverging infrastructure philosophies across different interfaces.
Next, the nature of platform capabilities will be explained as well as how to package Ansible execution as a promise.
Following that, the requirements for the solution will be defined, grouped into functional and non-functional requirements.

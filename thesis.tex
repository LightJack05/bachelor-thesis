\chapter*{Conventions}

\section*{Gender neutral language}
For the sake of readability and inclusivity, this thesis uses gender-neutral language throughout.
The singular \enquote{they/them} is used to refer to individuals whose gender is unspecified or not relevant to the discussion.

\section*{Differentiating object types from regular nouns}
Due to the naming conventions of objects in Kubernetes and similar systems, it may be difficult to determine whether \enquote{service} refers to a Kubernetes \enquote{Service} object, or a \enquote{service} offered by a third party or server etc. To remove ambiguity, the following convention is used in this thesis:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Object types as defined by Kubernetes, Ansible or other systems will be written in \texttt{PascalCase} with monospace fonts, such as \texttt{Service}, \texttt{Role} and \texttt{CustomResourceDefinition}.
	\item General nouns will be written in ordinary lowercase, such as a service offered by a platform or a host being a server.
\end{itemize}
